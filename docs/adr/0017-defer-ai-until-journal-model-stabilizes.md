# Defer AI until the journal model stabilizes

Superseded by ADR-0024. The completed futures Trade, Journal Entry, Playbook Assignment, Entry Time, Risk Dollars, and R Multiple model now exists.

MarketPilot will defer AI trade analysis until the new futures journal and playbook model is implemented. AI output should be based on playbook assignment, confluences, trade ideas, risk dollars, and R multiples rather than the old trade schema.
