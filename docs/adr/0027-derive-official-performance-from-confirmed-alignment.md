# Derive Official Playbook Performance from confirmed alignment

Official Playbook Performance is derived only from completed Trades with confirmed Playbook Alignment. Assigned, deviated, and unverified Trades remain visible and account-wide analytics still include every completed Trade, but assigned-trade results are labeled as a separate comparison. This supersedes ADR-0006's use of every assigned Trade for Playbook Performance while preserving its decision to derive metrics rather than persist them.
