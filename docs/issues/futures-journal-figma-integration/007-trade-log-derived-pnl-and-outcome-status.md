## What to build

Show derived Realized P&L and Outcome Status in the Trade Log. Realized P&L is calculated from risk dollars and R multiple. Outcome Status is derived as win, loss, or breakeven.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Trade Log shows derived Realized P&L for each completed Trade.
- [ ] Dense table cells display whole-dollar P&L.
- [ ] Outcome Status is derived from Realized P&L and shown as compact status pills.
- [ ] Positive and negative outcomes have clear Figma-style visual treatment.
- [ ] Visual test: capture a screenshot of rows with green/red P&L and status pills.
