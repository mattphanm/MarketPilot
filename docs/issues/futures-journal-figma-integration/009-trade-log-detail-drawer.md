## What to build

Clicking a Trade Log row opens a Figma-style right-side detail drawer. The drawer shows completed Trade facts plus joined Journal Entry context, including trade idea and confluences, without duplicating those fields in storage.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Clicking a Trade Log row opens a right-side drawer.
- [ ] Drawer shows entry time, symbol, direction, risk dollars, R multiple, Realized P&L, Outcome Status, and Playbook.
- [ ] Drawer shows trade idea and confluences from the Journal Entry.
- [ ] Drawer can be closed without losing table state.
- [ ] Visual test: capture a screenshot matching the Figma right-side drawer interaction.
