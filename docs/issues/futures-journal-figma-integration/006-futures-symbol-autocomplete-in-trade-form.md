## What to build

Add futures symbol autocomplete to the Trade form. Suggestions come from a curated futures symbol catalog grouped by category, but users may save custom symbols outside the catalog. Saved symbols are normalized to uppercase.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Trade form suggests known futures symbols as the user types.
- [ ] Catalog includes the agreed futures categories and symbols from the planning session.
- [ ] Users can save custom symbols not present in the catalog.
- [ ] Saved symbols are normalized to uppercase.
- [ ] Visual test: capture a screenshot of suggestions after typing `/M`.
